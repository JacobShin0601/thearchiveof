## Editorial choice

The July 2026 Samsung event is recent and unusually vivid, but the profit beat and stock decline do not identify a single cause. October 2025 is used for the rate-*cut* example; the September 2026 Fed decision was a hike, so describing that as a recent cut would be wrong. June CPI provides a clearer consensus surprise than the newer August headline reading, and is explicitly dated. All analogy diagrams are labelled conceptual; neither market price nor an investor's illustrated belief is a measured Bayesian posterior.
