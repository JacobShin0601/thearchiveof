## How to use it

The Base case assumes 2.6GW of 2030 deliveries and an 18.8% EBIT margin. Change product gross margin and operating expense ratio one at a time. Revenue growth alone does not guarantee an equivalent increase in FCFF or per-share value.

Then change deliveries. This is annual capacity actually delivered and recognized as revenue—not maximum option capacity in an announcement. The 4.0GW Bull case is above announced 2GW capacity, so it requires additional expansion, yield, and demand.

Finally, read the reverse result as an expectation test. It does not say the market literally forecasts one delivery number; it shows how large the combined assumptions must become if other inputs stay fixed.

## Limits

First-half operating cash flow includes material customer-deposit/deferred-revenue and payable movements. The model does not repeat those timing effects as perpetual cash flow. Stack life, project pricing, exact service contracts, and convertible-security details are not fully public, so precision should not be confused with certainty.
