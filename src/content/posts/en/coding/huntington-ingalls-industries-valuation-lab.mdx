This page makes the model behind the [Huntington Ingalls Industries analysis](/en/investing/markets/huntington-ingalls-industries-valuation/) interactive. The original question was not simply whether the U.S. Navy would order more ships. It was how much profit and cash HII could extract from orders already in hand.

The defaults reproduce the article’s Base case. Shipbuilding revenue grows 6% annually from the midpoint of 2026 guidance, Mission Technologies grows 5%, and Shipbuilding reaches a 7.5% operating margin in 2030. Those are author estimates, not company guidance.

## How to read the tool

Move Shipbuilding margin from 6% to 9% before changing anything else. Holding growth constant isolates what normalization does to EPS and fair value. Then change Shipbuilding growth to see the operating leverage if throughput improvements convert into revenue.

The exit P/E moves the answer quickly but does not explain the economics. Check the earnings bridge before raising the multiple. If the EPS and FCF methods diverge sharply, revisit the retirement-benefit, capital-spending, and working-capital assumptions.

## Model mechanics

Shipbuilding and Mission Technologies revenue compound for four years from the 2026 starting point.

$$
Revenue_{2030}=Revenue_{2026}(1+g)^4
$$

The model applies segment margins, then adjusts for non-segment items, interest, non-operating retirement benefits, and tax. It multiplies 2030 EPS by the selected exit P/E and discounts the result to the publication date.

$$
PV=\frac{EPS_{2030}\times Exit\ P/E}{(1+r)^{4.230}}
$$

The sensitivity table holds Mission growth and margin, the multiple, required return, and share count at the currently selected values. It changes only Shipbuilding growth and margin. Each cell is therefore a mechanical scenario result, not a probability forecast.

## What this model does not answer

- When program-level EAC adjustments or schedule delays will occur
- How contract type and incentive fees will affect realized margin
- How rates and pension-asset returns will change non-operating retirement income
- The annual path of capital spending and working capital
- The valuation multiple the market will assign in 2030

The point is not to produce one precise target price. It is to identify which assumption dominates the conclusion and re-test that assumption with each earnings release.
