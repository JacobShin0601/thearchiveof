This Lab turns the central question in the [Applied Digital Corporation research](/en/investing/markets/applied-digital-corporation-ai-data-center-value/) in both directions. It does not treat 1.41GW as value; it follows the route through rent, NOI, construction cost, capital claims, and common equity.

The decision is **Sell**. This is not a claim that the underlying business is poor; it is a judgment that the market price already requires substantially better execution and additional NAV than the preliminary Base case. The chart above updates only with closes after this decision.

## How to use it

In Simple mode, move one variable at a time. Raising residual CAPEX from \$12M/MW to \$13M/MW is often more informative than changing a terminal multiple: a small shift across a large development footprint can erase much of the residual common-equity claim.

In Advanced / Reverse mode, additional contracted MW represents an assumed positive-NPV project, not announced capacity. Explaining a market price with one variable is different from explaining it with a combination of lower CAPEX, lower discount rates, stronger re-letting, and incremental MW.

## The model boundary

The Lab does not present an arbitrary annual allocation of the disclosed “thereafter” rent as fact. Nor does it claim to have reconstructed every contractual cash distribution for Macquarie rights, NCI, convertibles, warrants, and other senior claims. False precision would be worse than an explicit uncertainty.

Its practical purpose is narrow: make visible what must improve beyond contract size for value to reach common shareholders.
