## Existing backlog and prepayments

We use the company's 36% / 38% / 26% recognition buckets, assume even allocations within them and assign the final bucket to years five through eight. A \$5 billion assumed prior collection, recognized evenly in years one and two, is discounted and subtracted from hypothetical unlevered cash flow. It is not a verified balance; individual customer cash timing is unknown.

## Renewals and new investment

Management identified more than 130,000 GPUs coming off contract in 2027 and approximately 170,000 in 2028. Annual renewal revenue equals GPU count × eligible fraction × 8,760 hours × paid utilization × realized hourly price. Cash begins in 2028 and 2029 respectively, for the selected duration and margin. GPU mix, realized prices and backlog overlap are not disclosed, so this is a scenario rather than a forecast.

New investments start in 2028 and produce unlevered annual cash beginning the following year. Annual cash yield is after operating costs, taxes and maintenance; initial investment is subtracted separately. New investment must exclude already contracted backlog and re-leased GPUs. Future cash flows—not historical book value—are discounted.

## Outside the model

The Base \$33.87 billion residual is the unmodeled net PV required to match the reference close, not an observed value or forecast. Future cohorts after the modeled period, salvage and omitted capital claims affect it. The 559M share input is a Q3 weighted-average proxy and excludes conversion dilution. Update assumptions when more disclosures are available. Subsequent price movement is information, not account return.
