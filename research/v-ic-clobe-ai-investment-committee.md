# V-IC (Clobe AI) — Series A Investment Committee Memo

**As of:** 2026-10-14  
**Recommendation:** Conditional  
**Decision rule:** Invest only after the data-request gate below is met and Pre-money is at or below KRW 2,100bn; prefer KRW 1,800–2,100bn.

## Thesis

V-IC can turn a free financial ERP into a differentiated distribution channel for SMB financing. If recurring operating data improves underwriting and borrowers repeat, software acquisition cost can be spread across multiple financial transactions. The result could be a capital-light financial infrastructure business rather than a conventional free SaaS product.

## Confirmed operating evidence

| Item | Public evidence | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product pricing | Clobe AI and Clobe Connect are free for life; Clobe Finance charges per transaction. | ERP is an acquisition and data-collection layer, not subscription revenue. |
| Product structure | Finance product is a future-receivables securitization / assignment structure, with repayment over the collection period. | Legal form does not establish economic loss allocation. |
| Customer base | 10,000+ corporate customers disclosed in April 2026. | Not equivalent to MAU or finance conversion. |
| Finance activity | KRW 110bn cumulative finance supply disclosed in April 2026. | Cumulative volume is not annual volume or revenue. |
| Repeat usage | Company states average finance use of 3.7x. | Positive signal; cohort definition and denominator are unverified. |
| Funding | KRW 13.4bn Pre-A completed in April 2026. | Series A price remains undisclosed. |

## Return underwriting

Assume a KRW 20bn investment, 20% cumulative dilution, and a four-year hold.

| Pre-money | Exit proceeds | MOIC | IRR | Decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| KRW 200bn | KRW 79.4bn | 4.0x | 41% | Attractive if diligence clears |
| KRW 300bn | KRW 54.6bn | 2.7x | 29% | Below 30% hurdle |
| KRW 500bn | KRW 33.6bn | 1.7x | 14% | Pass |

Base case maximum Pre-money is KRW 286bn at a 30% IRR hurdle and KRW 208bn at a 40% hurdle. A 30% further margin of safety implies approximately KRW 180–210bn.

## Strongest upside case

The free ERP can create a proprietary, longitudinal operating-data set that improves approval speed and loss rates. If the company retains fee economics while external capital bears most credit risk, a 45% operating margin and a 22x exit P/E make a substantially higher entry price supportable.

## Strongest downside case

The company may economically bear more credit risk than the product’s legal “assignment” language suggests. A rise in loss rate, cost of capital, or collection duration can absorb the customer discount quickly. A 2027 “KRW 100bn revenue” statement could also mean finance volume or annualized run rate rather than accounting revenue.

## Investment conditions precedent

1. Monthly 2024–2026 finance volume, average outstanding balance, and company-recognized revenue bridge.
2. Cohort-level ERP-to-finance conversion, repeat use, and contribution profit.
3. Vintage-level delinquency, gross loss, recoveries, and net loss by origination month.
4. Funding contracts and waterfall: seniority, first-loss, guarantees, recourse, and Clobe’s co-investment.
5. Audited or reviewed statements plus gross/net revenue recognition policy.
6. 2027 KRW 100bn revenue bridge with the definition of revenue and each bottom-up driver.
7. Current cap table, preferred rights, ESOP pool, and proposed Series A terms.
