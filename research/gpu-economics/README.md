RouteLLM is evidence for conditional task routing, not evidence of demand for older GPUs.

## Matched endpoint extension

Run `python3 panel.py /path/to/rent_panel_monthly.csv` with pandas installed. The exact pinned upstream panel is required. The script retains only unflagged catalog snapshots or price-list versions with nonnegative staleness at most 30 days, matches September 2024 and September 2026 on GPU, form factor, segment, provider, region, source and the complete instance-types field, and asserts one-to-one matching. Results are `results/matched_endpoints_2024_2026.csv` and `results/provider_endpoint_summary.csv`. It yields 38 provider/region/product rows across 3 providers, not 38 independent firms; same instance-types bundle does not guarantee each constituent SKU was unchanged. The filter is applied only to endpoints; intermediate missingness and future price paths are not inferred. The original example has a different window and retains older snapshots. Listing prices are not contracts or observations of inventory availability. Upstream data and included derivative rows: Rahil Mittal, GPU Price History, commit ee8820dd90d7715d8bb57cee03fb0882706d76c5, CC BY 4.0; underlying source terms also apply.

The scenario links hypothetical equal-quality inference break-even price to initial rent: $4 × 40/120 = $1.3333/hour. With the default cost and renewal assumptions, on-demand NPV is -$46,871 and take-or-pay is -$39,016. The corresponding NPV-zero initial prices are $3.8899 and $2.9439/hour, respectively. This calculation assumes fixed annual operating and maintenance expenses even if renewal fails, a zero tax rate, linear revenue and the exact unrounded inference threshold. It is not a CoreWeave estimate.

## Existing-asset continuation versus immediate sale

`continuation_value` values an installed asset at the end of its initial contract using future unlevered cash flows; it deliberately excludes the sunk acquisition cost. Default inputs assume three remaining years, renewal price $1.65/hour, paid utilization 70%, a single 70% renewal probability scaling all three years' revenue, 12% annual price decline, $7,500 fixed annual cash costs even without renewal, 12% discount rate and $3,000 year-three terminal proceeds. The value is -$682.34. Compared with illustrative net sale proceeds today of $4,000, sale is preferable; the continuation rent that makes the two equal is $2.1584/hour. Alternative uses, taxes, financing, working capital, flexibility and early retirement are excluded. A negative value of continuing reflects unavoidable fixed costs only if the operator chooses continuation; immediate sale avoids those costs. This is not an estimate of a CoreWeave asset.
